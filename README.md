# Hey, I'm Utkarsh 👋

I’m an investor and operator interested in how technology becomes durable businesses. I like working at the intersection of AI, software, financial systems, and the real constraints that decide whether an idea works outside a slide deck.

Most of my professional work has lived inside institutions and investment teams, so this GitHub is where I build and share in public.

Right now: Senior Associate at Duke Capital Partners and an MBA candidate at Duke University’s Fuqua School of Business.

Previously: quantitative portfolio-strategy research and advisory at Barclays; early-stage fund building at Elements Asset Management; and product management in digital banking at ICICI Bank.

## Side Projects

Each project is an attempt to make a complex system more legible:

- [**Explore Abundance**](https://www.utkarshruhela.com/ai-infrastructure) — An interactive field guide to AI infrastructure: from sand and silicon to the compute architectures that make modern intelligence possible.
- [**HistoryFlow**](https://history.utkarshruhela.com/) — An interactive historical atlas for tracing people, places, and events across time instead of consuming history as a static timeline.
- [**Indus Valley Glyphs**](https://www.utkarshruhela.com/indus-explorer) — An experimental visual workbench for browsing, comparing, and composing signs from the still-undeciphered Indus script.
- [**Benchmarking Local Inference**](https://www.utkarshruhela.com/benchmarking-local-inference) — A production-minded evaluation of a local 8B model across workflow tasks, tool routing, latency, and failure modes.
